REFUND POLICY

Try it. If it is not right, return it.

Effective date: August 15, 2026

14-day refund window

VoiceCue Personal purchases are eligible for a full refund when requested within 14 calendar days of the original purchase date.

How to request a refund

Reply to your purchase receipt or email hello@voicecue.app from the email address used at checkout. Include your order number where possible. We may deactivate the associated license after a refund is issued.

How refunds are issued

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method through our checkout partner. Processing time depends on the payment method and issuing bank.

Beta builds

Free beta builds have no purchase price and therefore do not require a refund. We welcome beta feedback at hello@voicecue.app.
