PRIVACY POLICY

Private by design.

Effective date: August 15, 2026

What stays on your Mac

VoiceCue processes speech recognition locally on your Mac using bundled on-device software. Your scripts, drafts, Script Library, and microphone audio are stored and processed locally. VoiceCue does not upload these materials to a VoiceCue cloud service.

Purchase and license data

If you purchase VoiceCue, our checkout partner may process the information required to complete the payment, send your receipt, and deliver or manage your license. VoiceCue does not receive or store your full payment-card details. A future license activation service may process only the minimum information needed to verify a purchase and manage device activations.

Support

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to include in that message. Please do not send scripts, recordings, license keys, or other sensitive material unless it is necessary for support.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as VoiceCue evolves. Material changes will be published on this page. For privacy questions, contact hello@voicecue.app.
